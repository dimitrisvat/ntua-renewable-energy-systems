\# Renewable Energy Systems - MATLAB Project



MATLAB simulations and analysis developed for the Renewable Energy Systems course at the National Technical University of Athens.



\## Overview



This repository contains MATLAB implementations for the analysis, modelling, and simulation of renewable energy systems, including wind energy, photovoltaic systems, and energy storage applications.



\## Project Structure



\### Section 1 - Wind Energy Systems



Includes:

\- Wind turbine power characteristics

\- Power coefficient analysis

\- Pitch control behaviour

\- Generator frequency response

\- Time-series simulations



\### Section 3 - Photovoltaic Systems



Includes:

\- PV performance analysis

\- Battery integration

\- Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE)

\- Net Present Value (NPV)

\- Internal Rate of Return (IRR)



\### Section 4 - Hybrid Renewable Energy Systems



Includes:

\- PV cost analysis

\- Financial evaluation

\- Battery sizing considerations



\## Technologies



\- MATLAB

\- Renewable energy modelling

\- Power systems analysis

\- Financial evaluation methods
