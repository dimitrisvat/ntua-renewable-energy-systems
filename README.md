# Renewable Energy Systems

Academic project developed at the **National Technical University of Athens (NTUA)** as part of the Renewable Energy Sources course.

The project covers the **technical and economic analysis of renewable energy systems and their integration into the electrical grid**.

## Highlights

- Wind turbine modelling, MPPT and pitch control
- PV plant grid-integration analysis
- Voltage and short-circuit studies
- Battery energy storage analysis
- Energy market and revenue analysis
- LCOE, NPV, IRR and payback period
- Residential PV and net billing

- MATLAB
